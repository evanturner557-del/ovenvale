Questions

Common questions

Do I need to be home?

Someone needs to let us in and be around at the end to check the oven. You don't need to watch.

Can you clean range cookers and AGAs?

Range cookers yes. For AGA-style heat-storage cookers, tell us the model first so we can check.

Do you clean the whole kitchen?

No. Ovens, hobs, extractors and microwaves only.
